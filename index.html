About

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Minnesota, advised by Stevie Chancellor in the GroupLens lab. My research sits at the intersection of human-computer interaction, ethical and human-centered machine learning, and social media platforms. It has three threads: the working conditions and mental health of the people who make AI and online platforms safe, including content moderators and data workers in Africa; how content moderation and language technologies fail communities that speak low-resource languages; and how machine learning models for high-risk mental health behaviors perform across cultures.

I work closely with Timnit Gebru, Zeerak Talat, Milagros Miceli, Dylan Baker, Alex Hanna, and others at DAIR on the impact of social media in communities that platforms neglect, and on the mental health of data workers. My work has appeared at CHI, FAccT, AIES, ACL, and NAACL, and has been covered by TIME, CNN, and The Guardian. I am a 2026 Doctoral Dissertation Fellow.

Before Minnesota, I completed a B.Sc. in Information Systems at Mekelle University (2020) and co-organized the Development working group at Mechanism Design for Social Good. Outside of work I play and watch European football, run (I recently finished a half-marathon), and play in a league organized by Minneapolis Recreation & Parks, so come talk football and running with me.
